# Quickstart

1. Restart `npm start` after this update.
2. Hover the left navigation and choose **Tự động số điện thoại**.
3. Turn it on, choose **Đã có số**, and save.
4. From a customer identity, send a valid number to CRM outside a campaign.
5. Confirm the contact and sidebar status change to **Đã có số**; open the filter and select that status.
6. Turn the rule off and repeat: the phone remains captured but the status does not change.

Do not test by sending a number from CRM itself: outgoing messages are intentionally excluded.
